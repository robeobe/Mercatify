Section 01

Executive summary

  • 1.1

    Five of your seven tools are covered by the platform you would run anyway

    HubSpot, Airtable, Sortly, Jobber and Zendesk together cost $2,043 a month. Every capability Voltix actually uses in them maps onto an Open Mercato module — three of them natively, two with configuration only. None of the five requires a bespoke module.

  • 1.2

    One genuine gap: the quote builder

    Generating a customer quote from a panel-and-inverter configuration is the only thing Voltix does that the platform does not already do. We estimate 40 hours. It is the single largest piece of new code in the programme, and it is a small one.

  • 1.3

    Two things we recommend you keep

    Xero stays: statutory books and VAT filing are not a platform problem, and replacing an accounting system to save $78 a month is a bad trade. Legally binding e-signature stays with PandaDoc, wired into the platform through its API rather than rebuilt.

  • 1.4

    The HubSpot contract sets the timetable

    Your HubSpot term renews on 28 February 2027 and is the only annual commitment in the stack. A programme starting in October reaches the CRM cut-over in week 10 — comfortably before the renewal decision, which is the point of sequencing it second rather than first.

  • 1.5

    Four usage statements we could not map

    Panel-layout design, utility interconnection filings, subsidy paperwork and payroll export sit outside our catalog. They are listed in Appendix B rather than folded into a percentage. Two of them may turn out to be real scope; we would rather find that out with you than around you.

What this report is not

It is not a proposal to replace your stack. It is an ordered list of pieces worth cutting, each with what it saves and what it costs to cut. You can stop after any wave and keep the savings banked to that point.

Rule 01

The catalog decides, the model narrates

Tool-to-platform verdicts come from a curated matrix by lookup, never from live model reasoning on the critical path. Language models write the explanations; they do not cast the votes.

Rule 02

The model never computes money

It selects the inputs. A pure function returns the figure. Every number in section 05 can be recomputed by hand at the table, which is why the formula is shown next to each line.

Rule 03

Confidence is a band, not a decimal

High — observed directly in your system or invoices. Medium — inferred from consistent evidence. Low — stated once, unverified. A "87% match" is a claim nobody can defend in a room full of people who know their own business.

Every case, artifact and figure in this report is a record in an Open Mercato instance — the same platform this report recommends. Mercatify runs its own business on it.
